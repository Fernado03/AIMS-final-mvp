# AIMS Pitch - Quick Reference Guide
## 🎯 30-Second Elevator Pitch

"AIMS is an AI-powered medical scribe that cuts documentation time by 60%. Doctors record patient consultations, and our AI generates structured SOAP notes in under 2 minutes - what normally takes 15-20 minutes. We're targeting 11,000 Malaysian private clinics with a RM150M market opportunity. Think Dragon Medical meets ChatGPT, but 70% cheaper and built for Malaysian healthcare."

### Slide 2: Solution (30 sec)
- Show landing page
- "AIMS uses AI to automate clinical documentation"

### Slide 3: Live Demo - Upload Audio (1 min)

- Edit if needed

### Slide 4: Enter Vitals (30 sec)
- BP: 140/90, HR: 95, Temp: 37.2°C
- Quick entry, click Next

### Slide 5: AI Assessment (1 min)
- Click "Generate Assessment"
- AI suggests: "Hypertension, rule out ACS"
- Show differential diagnoses

### Slide 6: AI Plan (1 min)
- Click "Generate Plan"
- AI recommends: ECG, medications, follow-up
- Approve and save

### Slide 7: Final Summary (30 sec)
- Show complete SOAP note
- Export to PDF
- "From 20 minutes to 2 minutes"

## 🛡️ Handling Tough Questions

### "How is this different from Dragon Medical?"
→ "Dragon is expensive (RM15K/year) and only does transcription. We're 70% cheaper AND provide AI-generated clinical insights. Plus, we support Malay and understand Malaysian medical context."

### "Why would doctors trust AI with medical notes?"
→ "They don't have to! The doctor is always in control. AI suggests, doctor decides. Every note is reviewed and approved by the clinician before saving. We're an assistant, not a replacement."

### "What about data security?"
→ "Phase 2 includes end-to-end encryption, PDPA compliance, and Google Cloud's enterprise security. For MVP, we're focused on functionality, but security is our top priority for production."

### "Can you make money from this?"
→ "Yes! If we save 1.5 hours/day, that's worth RM4,500-9,000/month to a doctor. Our price is RM500/month - clear ROI. With just 50 clinics, that's RM300K annual revenue."

### "What if Google shuts down your API?"
→ "Multi-cloud strategy. We can switch to AWS or Azure. Also exploring open-source alternatives like Whisper and Llama. We're not locked into one provider."

## 🎨 Body Language & Delivery Tips

### ✅ DO:
- **Make eye contact** - Especially when stating key numbers
- **Pause after big claims** - Let "60% time savings" sink in
- **Use hand gestures** - Show workflow: "Record → AI → Review → Approve"
- **Smile when talking about impact** - Show passion for solving burnout
- **Point to demo** - "Let me show you how this works"

### ❌ DON'T:
- Read from slides
- Apologize for being students
- Rush through demo
- Use too much technical jargon
- Forget to breathe!
